### 1. SAFTER: Safety-Aware Filtering Through Explicit Recoverability for Robust Reinforcement Learning under Distribution Shift
- **SOP Score:** 8
- **SOP 评分证据:** Venue +0 | Institution +0 | Keywords +8 | Hardware +0 | Code +0 | cs.RO +0
- **Keywords:** quadruped, reinforcement learning
- **Zotero:** 待入库
- **Abstract:** Learning safe policies that generalize to unseen environments is difficult under bounded actuation, where an unsafe decision may become unrecoverable. Conventional control barrier function (CBF) filters enforce only instantaneous safety and ignore future recoverability. We propose SAFTER (Safety-Aware Filtering Through Explicit Recoverability), which embeds a differentiable, closed-form backup-CBF filter into Soft Actor-Critic training.
